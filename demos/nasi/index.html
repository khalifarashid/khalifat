Things parents ask first

How much of this do I have to do with them?

Two of the five activities are built for a child to do alone. The make-it kit is nicer with an adult, but the instructions are drawn, not written, so a seven year old can follow them.

My child speaks no Arabic. Is that a problem?

No. Everything is written in both languages and the word cards start from zero. Plenty of our families speak Tagalog, Malayalam or Russian at home.

What if the box is wrong for my child?

Tell us within a week and the next one is adjusted, or refunded. You keep the box either way, we are not asking anyone to repack glue.

Do you do parties and gatherings?

Yes, and they are separate from the monthly box. We bring the activity, the table setup and enough mess mats for up to twenty children. Send us the date on WhatsApp and we will come back with what fits the age group.

Where do you deliver?

All seven emirates, free. Deliveries land Tuesday to Thursday so the box is there before the weekend.
